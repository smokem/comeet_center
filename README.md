Co.meet Space is a professional training center based in Sfax, Tunisia, offering short courses
in management, communication, digital skills, and office tools — in-person, hybrid, or online,
with groups capped at 15 participants and instructors who still practice their field.

This repository is the public website and admin platform:
- SSR-rendered catalog and course detail pages (TanStack Start + Nitro)
- Live business hours and coming-soon gate backed by Firestore
- Contact form via EmailJS, WhatsApp-first booking flow
- Admin dashboard for full course CRUD, session management, and content editing
- SEO layer: JSON-LD (LocalBusiness, Course, FAQPage), sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt
- Cloudinary image pipeline with responsive srcset and blur placeholders
- Deployed on Vercel with NITRO_PRESET=vercel
